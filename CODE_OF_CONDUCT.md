# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Offer specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and disclosure of private traffic or credentials are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation according to severity and repetition. They should explain decisions privately where possible and apply this policy consistently. Reports involving security vulnerabilities should follow SECURITY.md. Reports of conduct problems should be directed privately to repository maintainers through a contact channel configured when the project is published; do not publish private information in an issue. Maintainers must provide that channel before opening a public community.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and project-associated community spaces. Good-faith disagreement about technical choices is welcome.
